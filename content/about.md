_AI @ RRCHNM_ publishes critical reflections, practical tutorials, reusable templates, and short field notes about generative AI in historical research and teaching. We are neither boosters nor abolitionists. Our goal is to help historians make informed decisions about using these tools, and about declining to use them. This is edited at the [Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media](https://rrchnm.org/) at George Mason University.

## What we publish

- **Essays:** arguments and reflections on what AI changes, and what it doesn't.
- **Tutorials:** reproducible workflows with verification built in.
- **Templates:** prompts, policies, and syllabus language you can adapt.
- **Field Notes:** short reports from experiments, classrooms, and archives.

Interested in writing for us? See [Contribute](/contribute/).
