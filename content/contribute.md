Send a short pitch, one or two paragraphs, describing your piece and which type it fits: essay, tutorial, template, or field note. Tell us what readers will be able to think or do after reading it.

## Guidelines

- **Essays** run 1,500 to 4,000 words.
- **Tutorials** should be reproducible and include a verification step.
- **Templates** should explain the context they were written for.
- **Field Notes** run 500 to 1,200 words.

Pitches go to *editors@example.org* (placeholder address).
