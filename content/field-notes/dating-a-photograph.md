We gave four multimodal models the same undated photograph of a city street and asked each one when it was taken. The answers ranged from "about 1905" to "the mid-1940s." Each came with a confident explanation.{{< sidenote >}}Invented experiment for the prototype.{{< /sidenote >}}

The models noticed the right *kinds* of evidence: vehicles, clothing, signage. They disagreed about what that evidence meant. None of them noticed the one detail a curator pointed out right away, which was a theater marquee advertising a film whose release date anyone could look up.

{{< pullquote >}}Models are good at listing evidence. They are much less good at knowing which piece of evidence settles the question.{{< /pullquote >}}

**Takeaway:** use a model to generate a list of features to check, then do the dating yourself.
