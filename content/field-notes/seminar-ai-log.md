Last spring, a graduate methods seminar kept a shared spreadsheet. Every time a student used a generative AI tool for coursework, they logged what they asked, what they got, and whether it helped.{{< sidenote >}}Fictional seminar for the prototype.{{< /sidenote >}}

By the end of the semester there were 214 entries. A few patterns stood out:

- **Translation help** was the most common use, and students rated it the most useful.
- **Finding sources** was the least reliable. About a third of suggested references could not be verified.
- **Drafting** declined over the semester, as students said the drafts "sounded like no one."

The log itself turned out to be the most valuable part. Writing down each use made students more deliberate about it.
