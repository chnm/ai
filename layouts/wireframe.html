Footer
  1. Title and lede. No banner or cover image; the headline and italic lede sit on the paper background.
  2. Metadata. Type, authors (ORCID via data/authors.toml), date, DOI, and tutorial details sit in the left margin at 1300px and up, and as a row under the lede on smaller screens.
  3. Reading column. 38rem wide, with a drop cap in the type color.
  4. Sidenotes. {{ "{{< sidenote >}}" }} places a note in the right margin at 1300px and up, and inline below the paragraph on smaller screens. Ordinary Markdown footnotes ([^1]) also appear in the margin at 1300px and up, and stay in the numbered list at the end of the piece.
  5. Quotations. Markdown > blockquotes are body-size with a thin rule in the type color. {{ "{{< pullquote >}}" }} sets a large display quote, and {{ `{{< epigraph source="…" >}}` }} sets an indented opening quotation with an optional source.
  6. Prompt box. {{ "{{< prompt title=\"…\" >}}" }} renders a copyable block, used mostly in Templates.
  7. Cite this. A generated Chicago citation. Highwire citation_* meta tags let Zotero save the piece as a journal article.
  8. More in this section. Up to three other pieces of the same type, below the article.
